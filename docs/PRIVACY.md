# YachtPA privacy policy (draft)

Last updated: 2026-09-25. Draft for review before publishing; Play requires a public URL for this text.

**What YachtPA is.** A logbook for yacht crew. It records voyages and daily work so you can produce a sea-service record.

**Location.** YachtPA records your position only between the moment you tap *Start voyage* and the moment you tap *Stop voyage*. While a voyage is running the app keeps recording in the background, so that a passage is logged even when your phone is locked or in your pocket. Outside a voyage no location is collected. Positions are stored on your phone. They are used to compute distance, duration, night hours and start and end places, and to draw the track of the voyage. They are not sold, shared with advertisers, or used for anything else.

**Other data.** Vessel names, positions held, dates, certificates and log entries you enter are stored on your phone. Voice notes and transcripts, when you use them, are processed to produce a log entry you confirm; nothing is saved that you did not confirm.

**Sync and backup.** When cloud backup is switched on (not yet available), the same data is copied to your account so you can restore it on another phone. It stays yours: you can export everything and delete your account and all its data from inside the app.

**Sharing.** Nothing is shared unless you choose to: for example when you send a PDF of your record to a captain or an employer.
